Part 2 · Service delivery readiness

What's needed to actually deliver on the site's claims

The marketing is ahead of the operation. These are the real pieces — and yes, infrastructure is required; "no new hardware" is only half-true.

Blocker Cannot take a single client without these
A1

Choose a PSTN calling model

Blocker

Teams alone can't dial real phone numbers — the entire value prop. Pick one: Microsoft Calling Plans (MS is carrier), Operator Connect (3rd-party carrier, no hardware), or Direct Routing (your own SIP trunk + a certified Session Border Controller — real telecom hardware/cloud infra). This single choice drives cost, pricing, porting, and whether "no new hardware" is even true.

A2

Microsoft CSP partner + GDAP admin

Blocker

To configure a client's tenant you need delegated admin via Granular Delegated Admin Privileges. Becoming a Cloud Solution Provider also lets you resell licenses — the actual source of recurring margin. Without it you're trading admin passwords, which is unacceptable for a medical client.

A3

BAA framework (HIPAA)

Blocker

Patient calls, voicemail transcripts, and chat are PHI. You must accept Microsoft's BAA and sign your own BAA with each practice — the moment you hold admin access to a tenant with PHI, FixedIT Tech is a Business Associate. This is a legal prerequisite, not a feature. The site markets to medical offices and says nothing about it.

A4

E911 / dynamic emergency addresses

Blocker

Kari's Law and RAY BAUM'S Act legally require that a 911 call from Teams routes with the correct location — per office, for a multi-location practice (the exact target). Misconfiguration is illegal and a life-safety risk. Must be in every rollout.

Required Needed to operate honestly & reliably
B1

Number porting process

Required

"Existing numbers ported over" = porting orders, Letters of Authorization, carrier coordination, and 2–4 week timelines with temporary numbers in between. A real workstream, not a checkbox.

B2

Support model + monitoring + SLA

Required

For a medical office, downed phones = patients can't reach the practice = clinical and liability risk. "Ongoing support" needs a ticketing/helpdesk, escalation path, after-hours expectations, and call-quality/uptime monitoring (Teams Call Quality Dashboard).

B3

Provisioning / offboarding runbooks

Required

The site promises same-day offboarding and clean lifecycle management. Across clients, documentation alone won't hold — you need PowerShell/Graph runbooks (or an identity tool) to deliver provisioning, role changes, and same-day access revocation reliably.

B4

Security & compliance baseline

Required

MFA / Conditional Access, audit logging, and retention policies for voicemail transcripts and any call recordings, plus controls on who can access PHI-bearing data. Clients and their cyber insurers will ask.

Will surface Comes up on the first sales call
C1

A fax answer

Will surface

Medical offices run on fax (referrals, scripts, insurance) and Teams has no native fax. Replacing the phone system invites "what about our fax line?" — needs a HIPAA-compliant cloud-fax (eFax) answer.

C2

Honesty corrections to the copy

Will surface

Caveat "no new hardware / uses your existing licenses" (Teams Phone is an ~$8/user/mo add-on plus calling cost plus resource-account licenses for auto-attendants/queues). And "skill-based queues" is really one-queue-per-skill, not a true skills engine — don't oversell it.

Top of the list

Decide the PSTN model (A1) and stand up the CSP + BAA path (A2/A3) — those are what legally and technically allow a first medical client.
